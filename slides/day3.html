Thresholds

Rates across groups need a tolerance; duties to each person do not

Fairness: ten points

Rates move by chance across small groups. The gap is judged against a threshold set in advance.

Transparency: zero

Each guardian is owed the notice. One reply without it is one guardian misled.

What goes wrong. A threshold written after the results are known is fitted to pass.

Ask the room for an example of each kind in their own work. A useful extra point: with twelve replies per language, one reply moves the rate by more than eight points; a real test set needs more cases than this classroom one.

How to test for a language gap

Ask the same thing twice, once in each language

  • Twenty enquiries guardians send, each in English and in Arabic.
  • Arabic in the forms guardians write: Modern Standard and Gulf.
  • Twelve routine questions with a known answer; eight sensitive ones that must reach a person.
  • Same guardian, same child, same question: the only difference is the language.

A scripted stand-in gives everyone the same numbers

Placing Wafi

Limited risk, on one condition

  • Wafi answers factual questions; it decides nothing about a child.
  • Health, safety, custody, complaints and disputed fees are meant to reach a person first.
  • So the principles apply, and Wafi is tested against them.

The condition

If sensitive replies went out without review, or Wafi began to decide anything about a child, it would be high risk. Lab 3.2 showed the routing does not yet hold in Arabic.

This is the course's placement, and reasonable people may argue for high: minors' data at scale. The capstone review often changes this fact. The stretch of Lab 3.3 asks what follows if replies are sent without review.
