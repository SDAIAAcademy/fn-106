Full marks, one example each. Data protection: the before-and-after field list, the pseudonymisation code and a re-identification rate measured on it, a retention rule as code. Classification and sharing: six sets with level, reason and destination, and the sharing conditions. Risk: a tier with reasons and a register with owners, dates and residual ratings. Ethics tests: the three checks and the forty-enquiry rows, with thresholds set first. Format

Ten minutes each: six to present, four for a changed fact

1 · The build

Whose data, what it does, where prompts go. One slide or the README.

2 · The pack

Walk the repository: registers, code, test report, records, notice. Show the numbers.

3 · The changed fact

The assessor changes one fact; you revise the classification and risk entries live.

Peer feedback. After each review, everyone writes one strength, one question and one risk they would add.

Changed facts to choose from, one per participant: a new field (a child's photo, a medical certificate); a new recipient (a transport contractor, a ministry report); a hosted model in another country for real data; sensitive replies sent without review; a data subject asks for deletion; a second breach. Keep the list private before the day. The commit history of each repository is inspected for authenticity.
